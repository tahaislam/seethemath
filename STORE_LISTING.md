## Full description (4000)

```
SeeTheMath turns the math concepts that trip kids up into clear, interactive pictures.
Instead of memorizing rules, students SEE why the math works — one step at a time.

Every topic has two modes:
• Guided Examples — the solution is revealed step by step, with the diagram building as you go.
• Try It Yourself — change the numbers and watch the visualization update live.

TOPICS (organized by the Ontario math strands)

Number
• Multiplying Fractions — area models and number lines, from integer × fraction to mixed × mixed.
• Decimals & Percents — hundreds grids and bar models connect fractions, decimals, and percentages.

Financial Literacy
• Income Tax Brackets — watch income flow through Canadian federal and Ontario provincial brackets, one slice at a time, and see why a "marginal rate" isn't your whole paycheck. (Educational only.)

Spatial Reasoning
• Dilations — scale shapes up and down from a center point.
• Pythagorean Theorem — build a square on each side of a right triangle and see a² + b² = c².

WHY PARENTS AND TEACHERS LIKE IT
• Built around understanding, not drills.
• No ads. No accounts. No tracking. Nothing is collected — it all runs on the device.
• Free and open source.
• Works offline.

Made for curious students, supportive parents, and busy teachers. See the math. Get the math.
```
